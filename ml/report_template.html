MesoNet track verification

A U-Net reads MRMS composite reflectivity and 0–2 km and 3–6 km merged AzShear every ~4 min and marks mid-level mesocyclone centers. A tracker links those into mesocyclone tracks and groups them into supercells. It was trained on 22 days of 2022 tracks, its tracker was tuned on 3 held-out 2022 days, and it was then run unchanged on the 5 days of 2023.

Scores

Pooled over days. A hand-made meso is found when a predicted meso is within 5 km of it in at least half of its frames. A predicted meso is a false alarm when it matches a hand-made meso in fewer than half of its frames and lies mostly inside the seven-state truth region (predicted tracks outside it are not scored). CSI = found / (hand-made + false).
